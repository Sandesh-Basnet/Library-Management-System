History of this Tome

Recovered from the flooded remains of the Crescent Observatory in 1892, this volume remains the only comprehensive guide to the shifting borders of the Dreamlands. Every page is hand-vellum and infused with a low-level resonance that allows the ink to remain visible even in total darkness.

Scholars believe Alistair Vane did not write these words, but rather listened to them. The text describes landscapes that no longer exist and stars that have never been seen by the waking eye. Note the coffee stains on page 412—bequeathed by the original curator who reportedly vanished while studying the central fold-out map.
